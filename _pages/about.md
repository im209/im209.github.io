I am **Kyeongkyu Lee**, an M.S. student in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at **Inha University**, South Korea.

My research focuses on multimodal learning and large language models. I am particularly interested in building reliable AI systems and exploring how language-model-based agents can communicate and collaborate efficiently.

## Research Interests

- **LLM Agents** — Multi-agent collaboration, evaluation, and communication.
- **Multimodal AI** — Vision-language models and medical image understanding.
- **Efficient LLMs** — Efficient inference, KV-cache sharing, and latent communication.

## Education & Affiliation

- **M.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering** (in progress), Inha University.
- **Machine Intelligence Lab**, Inha University.
